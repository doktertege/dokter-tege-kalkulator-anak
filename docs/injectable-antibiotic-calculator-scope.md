# MEDORA Injectable Antibiotic Calculator Notes

This document records the scope and limitations of the pediatric injectable-antibiotic calculators.

## Ceftriaxone

- Age scope: 2 months through 18 years; body weight under 50 kg.
- Standard non-meningitis reference: 50–80 mg/kg/day IV once daily, maximum 4 g/day, based on the Indonesian Pediatric Urology Guideline (IAUI, 2022), Table 20.3.
- Bacterial meningitis reference: 100 mg/kg/day, divided every 12 hours, maximum 4 g/day.
- Neonates are deliberately excluded. Ceftriaxone has important neonatal bilirubin and calcium-containing IV solution restrictions. Do not apply this calculator to premature neonates or neonates with jaundice/hyperbilirubinemia, or those receiving calcium-containing IV solutions.

## Cefotaxime

- Age scope: 2 months through 12 years; body weight under 50 kg.
- Standard reference: 50–180 mg/kg/day divided into 4–6 doses; maximum 12 g/day.
- Meningitis reference: 200–300 mg/kg/day divided every 4–6 hours; maximum 2 g per dose and 12 g/day.
- Neonates are deliberately excluded because dosing interval depends on postnatal age and prematurity.
- Dose selection must be matched to indication, culture/susceptibility, renal function and local antimicrobial guidance.

## Gentamicin

- Age scope: 3 months through 18 years; body weight under 50 kg.
- Calculates an initial 7 mg/kg dose only.
- It does not calculate a repeat dose or interval. Subsequent administration requires renal-function assessment and therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), according to local protocol.
- Neonatal gentamicin dosing is deliberately excluded because dosing depends on birth weight, gestational age, postnatal age and renal status.

## Shared safety limits

- These calculators do not diagnose infection or select empiric antibiotic therapy.
- They do not replace local antibiograms, hospital antimicrobial guidelines, culture results, allergy assessment, renal/hepatic assessment or specialist input.
- Reconstitution and final concentration for ceftriaxone/cefotaxime are not calculated because products and diluents differ. Always follow the exact product insert and institutional preparation protocol.
